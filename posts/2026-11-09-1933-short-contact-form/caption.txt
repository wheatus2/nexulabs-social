Plenty of contact forms ask for more than they need.

Company name, budget, full address, how did you find us. Google's web.dev guide to address and payment forms is blunt: don't ask for data you don't need. For a first enquiry, a name, a way to reply and what they're after is usually enough.

Then make it easy on a phone. Put a label above each box, as grey hint text vanishes once you type. Set the phone field as a phone number so the keypad comes up, and switch autocomplete on.

Tonight, try your own form on your phone.
