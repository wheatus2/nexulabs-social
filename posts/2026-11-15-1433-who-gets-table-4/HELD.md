## What to take from it
- Two flat cards on a plain sweep is too little object for four scenes; the 3D is clean but there is nothing to look at for long. A busier real still life (a table laid for two, cutlery, a glass) or one big close-up would carry more.
- The hook has to make the clash visible at thumbnail size: the two 19:30s large, side by side, not two small cards.
- Each scene must move closer or change state; a pair of frames with the same layout reads as filler.
- If the payoff says "one diary", show prevention (the website already showing 19:30 as taken), not a fix after the event.
