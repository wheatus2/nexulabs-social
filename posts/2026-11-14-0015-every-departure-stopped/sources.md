## 1. On Friday 13 November 2026 the FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop of all departing flights, "for reasons of safety"
- Used on: frames 1 and 3, caption.
- Source A (Reuters, carried by RTE): "US FAA grounds all departing flights due to extraordinary circumstances", https://www.rte.ie/news/world/2026/1113/1599187-us-flights/ , 13 Nov 2026, 21:25. It reports "a national ground stop of all departing flights", that "flights in the air may land, but flights on the ground cannot take off", and that international flights "will be managed on a case-by-case basis". It quotes the FAA: "All departing traffic should remain groundstopped until further notice."
- Source B (Associated Press, carried by PBS News): "FAA grounds all flights at U.S. airports due to air traffic control communications problems", https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/faa-grounds-all-flights-at-u-s-airports-due-to-air-traffic-control-communications-problems , 13 Nov 2026, 4:41 PM EST. FAA notice as quoted: "due to extraordinary circumstances and for reasons of safety."
- Source C (CBS News): "FAA issues nationwide ground stop due to "extraordinary circumstances"", https://www.cbsnews.com/news/faa-issues-nationwide-ground-stop/ , 13 Nov 2026, 4:29 PM EST: "a rare nationwide ground stop on all departing flights".
- Source D (ABC News): "Nationwide ground stop ordered at airports: FAA", https://abcnews.com/US/nationwide-ground-stop-ordered-airports-faa/story?id=138121848 , 13 Nov 2026: ordered "for all airlines "due to extraordinary circumstances and for reasons of safety.""
- Scope note: some headlines say "all flights". We follow Reuters and CBS, which are the more exact: departures were stopped; planes already in the air could land. "Every departure in America" is that, in plain words.
- Primary source: the FAA's own notice and the transport secretary's post on X could not be opened from here. Both are quoted in the same words by AP, Reuters, CBS and ABC.

## 2. The cause given: "a communication issue" at all air route traffic control centres; FAA technicians investigating
- Used on: frame 2 ("THE COMMS FAILED" and the sentence under it), frame 3, caption.
- Source: CBS News (as above) and Yahoo News, "FAA issues nationwide ground stop on all departing flights, citing 'communication issue'", https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/article/faa-issues-nationwide-ground-stop-on-all-departing-flights-citing-communication-issue-213136811.html , updated 13 Nov 2026, 10:13 PM UTC. Both quote Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy: "issued a nationwide ground stop for all U.S. airports following a communication issue impacting all Air Route Traffic Control Centers. FAA technicians are investigating the issue."
- AP (PBS) describes "communications problems at regional air traffic control centers".
- "The comms failed" is our short form of "a communication issue impacting all Air Route Traffic Control Centers". We do not say it was a single fault, what failed or why: that had not been established. Only departures were stopped; planes in the air landed.

## Fact-check record
- First independent check (fresh subagent, about 23:50 UTC on 13 Nov 2026): the facts, quotes, date and attributions were confirmed against Reuters (RTE), AP (PBS), CBS News, ABC News and Yahoo News. Three things were changed because of it: "one" fault and "one line failed" (no source says it was a single failure) became "the comms failed"; "everything stopped" became "every departure stopped"; the FlightAware figure was taken out (a running day total, already out of date); "its" centres on frame 2 became "America's".
- Status when checked: not lifted in anything that could be opened; officials called it a telecommunications outage, said there was no indication of an attack, and had given no cause. The post says none of that either way.
