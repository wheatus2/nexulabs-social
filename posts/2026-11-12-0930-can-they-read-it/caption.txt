Web design check for North East businesses: is the text on your website too faint to read?

WebAIM tests the world's top million home pages every year. This February, 83.9% had low-contrast text. It was the most common accessibility fault they found.

The web standard, WCAG, asks for a contrast of at least 4.5 to 1 between body text and its background.

Take your phone outside and open your website. If the words wash out in daylight, make them darker. WebAIM's contrast checker is free and gives you the ratio for any two colours.
