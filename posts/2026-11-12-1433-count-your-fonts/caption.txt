Branding check for North East businesses: count your fonts.

Look at your website, your van, your invoice and your socials. If each one uses a different typeface, it's harder for people to recognise them as the same business.

Adobe's design guidance is to pick one or two fonts that work together and use the same ones across everything you put out. Apple gives app designers similar advice: keep the number of typefaces down, because mixing too many can make things harder to read.

So pick one. Two at most. Then use it everywhere.
