CRM for trades in the North East: when a job's finished, who asks the customer for a review?

If the answer is "whoever remembers", let the system ask. Mark the job done and a short message goes out by itself. The message in the picture is made up.

Two of Google's rules are worth knowing. Don't ask only the happy customers. And don't offer a discount or a freebie in return. The CMA's guidance for UK businesses that publish reviews also lists reviews that hide a reward as banned content.

Google's own tip is to give customers a link or QR code to leave one.
