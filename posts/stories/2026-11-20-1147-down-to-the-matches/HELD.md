## The idea
Branding for a made-up restaurant (Oxlip) on the things a table holds, rendered in Blender on an olive cloth: a cream Reserved card, a stack of coasters, a lilac matchbox, one flower mark on all three. "More than a logo." / "Down to the matches." (the tray pulled out: black tray, lilac match heads) / "Yours next. See what we build at nexulabs.co.uk".

## Review 1: 6.5 (frames 7, 6.5, 5.5)
Frame 3 was the lilac box behind a large blank white box: "an empty cream slab", "reads as an unfinished render", and it outweighed the call to action. Frame 2 had a blurred coaster stack under the headline (the second-largest object a blur). Objects sat in the bottom 320px in every frame. Label and call-to-action lines in two different styles.

## Review 2: 7 (frames 7, 7.5, 6)
Reworked: frame 3 as the Oxlip box in front and a white proof box behind it printed with a dashed circle and "Your name"; frame 2 as the open matchbox alone; everything above y 1600; one style for the small line. Palette, type and restraint were rated at big-brand level; the 3D "clean but simple CG". Frame 3 basic (borderline): the front box square on shows no side face and reads as "a 2D sticker" beside the box behind, which shows its side; "Your name" is grey on cream and only 40px tall, so the payoff is the faintest thing in the Story; the two boxes are 55px apart, neither overlapping nor clear.

## For next time
- A box square on to the camera is a flat rectangle. Turn every box so two faces show, and give a pair one perspective.
- The payoff print has to be the strongest thing in the last frame, not a pale proof: full ink, and as large as the sample beside it.
- The open matchbox (frame 2, 7.5) is the picture to build on: black tray, coloured heads, the mark on the sleeve. It wants wall thickness at the sleeve mouth and a shadow inside.
- Keep the top of the object at one height from frame to frame (it moved 760, 935, 785), and give every frame the small line under the headline or none.
- A matchbox corner just under a petal on the coaster was called a tangent twice: overlap boldly or clear it.
- Timings on four cores: about 4 minutes a 1080x1920 frame at 160 samples; drafts of all three in 45 seconds.
