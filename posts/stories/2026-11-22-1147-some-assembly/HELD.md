11:47 Story slot, Sunday 22 Nov 2026. 3 frames. Promotional, websites, "Book a call at nexulabs.co.uk".

The independent design review scored it 6.5 twice (pass mark 7) and the second review called frame 2 basic, so the scheduled run published nothing, as its instructions say. The Story is complete (slide-01 to slide-03, sources.md) and can be published as it stands (publish.yml, mode story, this folder) if the owner says so.

## The idea
A website as a plastic model kit, rendered in Blender: one sprue of ivory plastic on a violet table, the parts of a web page still on their runners (address bar, Home, About, Contact, a picture frame, lines of text, a Book now button, a pointer, a form field, Send) and the Nexulabs wordmark moulded into the maker's tab. "Some assembly required." / "Not by you." / "We build it. Book a call at nexulabs.co.uk" (the page put together, lying on its own empty sprue, the pointer on Book now). Unbounded 800 headlines.

## Review 1: 6.5 (frames 7, 5.5, 7)
Both reviewers said the idea and the three-beat line are good enough for a big brand. Frame 2 was a close-up of the top of the sprue with the Book now button set down beyond the rail: "nothing happens in it", no empty slot in view, the loose part 15px from the rail, parts sliced by the edges. Moulded lettering in Unbounded "doubled and smeared"; lighting flat, "a clay render"; headline word spaces too tight; the pointer over the w of "Book now".

## Review 2: 6.5 (frames 7, 5.5 basic, 6.5)
Reworked: moulded lettering in Geist Bold with no front fill light (much crisper), a strip light behind for highlights, the button moved to its own bay in the top row and shown cut loose there, the maker's tab moved to the foot, word spaces opened, pointer clear of the lettering. Frame 2 was still "a crop, not a composition": a zoom into frame 1 that shows no change of state. Still "one matte clay material, flat even lighting". Frame 3's page reached y 1680, under the reply box, and has no wordmark on it.

## For next time
- The middle frame needs an event, not a closer look: side cutters on a gate, a part in mid-air, a hand. A part lying loose in its own bay does not read as a change at Story size. If there is no event to show, make it two frames.
- One plastic colour on one flat sweep reads as a clay render. Give the kit a second material (a printed instruction sheet under it, a box lid, steel cutters) and a harder key with visible highlights along the runners.
- Moulded lettering needs a cap height of 25px or more and dark walls (no fill from the camera side). Geist Bold reads; Unbounded 800 does not.
- Keep the payoff object above y 1600 and put the wordmark on it.
- A close-up of a grid of parts slices something on every edge. Either frame whole rows, or build the close-up scene with only the parts that fit.
- Timings on four cores: about 3 minutes a 1080x1920 frame at 160 samples; drafts of all three in 45 seconds.
