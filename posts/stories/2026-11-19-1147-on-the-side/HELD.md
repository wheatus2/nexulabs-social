# Held: stories/2026-11-19-1147-on-the-side (19 Nov 2026, 11:47 run)

Not published. Two independent reviews, 6 and 6 out of 10 (pass mark 7), and frame 2 was called basic both times. The scheduled task says to publish nothing after two failed rounds, so nothing went out. The frames here are the second version.

The idea: hosting, domains and email as the sides on a cafe menu board. One plum felt letter board, push-in letters, oak frame, pink counter (Blender, art/build.py). On the menu: your new website / Sides: hosting, domains, email, same kitchen / To order: book a call at nexulabs.co.uk.

What both reviewers said
- Frame 2 (the list of three words) is basic whatever the camera does: square on it was "words with a drop shadow on stripes", angled it was "a default-font list" with nothing to anchor it. A frame that is only a stacked list of words needs an object or a change in it.
- Oswald Bold as push-in letters reads as Impact. The typeface was named as the biggest thing holding it back.
- Even, soft light: no highlight, no drama, plain plastic letters.
- Three cameras with no clear move between them read as three separate renders.
- Round 1: an address and wordmark set flat over a counter shot in perspective was "the most mistake-looking thing in the set". Round 2 put the address on the board as letters and the wordmark on the oak rail, which removed that complaint, but the address in small condensed capitals was called cramped, with the full stops nearly lost.
- A small label row sits too close to the headline under it once the board is seen at an angle (the end of "On the menu" ran into "Your"). Leave more than two slats.

What worked
- The idea and the palette (plum, ivory, one pink) were liked both times. Frames 1 and 3 scored 6.5.
- Felt slats as real geometry with a sheen material render in about 4 minutes a frame at 1080x1920 on four cores.
- Letters placed one glyph at a time from HarfBuzz positions, so the font's kerning is used. Blender draws Oswald at 0.74 of its em: measure a glyph before trusting a size.
