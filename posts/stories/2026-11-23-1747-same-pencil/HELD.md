# Held: not published (23 Nov 2026, 17:47 Story)

Website and CRM built as one, as a red-and-blue checking pencil rendered in 3D (Blender): "This end is your website." / "This end is your CRM." / "One pencil. Website and CRM, built as one. See what we build at nexulabs.co.uk".

Two independent design reviews, 6.5 both times (pass mark 7). No frame was called basic. The run followed the scheduled task's rule: two failed rounds, publish nothing.

- Round 1 (frames 7, 6.5, 6): the last frame showed only the join of the pencil, so neither point was in it ("a two-tone stick, not one pencil with two ends"), and it carried five things. Ticks loose and read as mirrored; "new" read as "neu".
- Round 2 (frames 7, 6.5, 5.5): the last frame remade as the whole pencil, point to point, in perspective. Marked down: the far point is a few pixels, the wordmark on the barrel is about 80px long and turned 60 degrees, the lacquer washed out next to frames 1 and 2 (more sky light was added between rounds), the shadow is a slab wider than the pencil, type top left and object right with a dead lower left.
- Both rounds: the handwriting "reads as a script typeface with a grain filter, not a pencil line" (even width, no pressure); the pencil is not where the writing stopped; the top shading looks dirty on a pale paper.

What was liked both times: the idea ("strong enough for that level"), the type and palette, frame 1 (the red point, 7).

To pick up again: art/build.py (pencil, cameras), art/print.py (foil stamp), art/page.py (handwriting). Renders take about 2 minutes a frame, drafts 9 seconds.
