## Claim

**A limited company's website has to show four things: the company's registered number, its registered office address, where the company is registered, and the fact that it is a limited company.**

- Source: GOV.UK, "Running a limited company: your responsibilities", section 6, "Signs, stationery and promotional material". URL: https://www.gov.uk/running-a-limited-company/signs-stationery-and-promotional-material (found by web search in this run and opened from that result, 13 Nov 2026; the page loaded normally).
- As the page states it, under "Stationery and promotional material": business letters, order forms and websites must show the company's registered number; its registered office address; where the company is registered (England and Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland); and the fact that it's a limited company, usually by spelling out the company's full name including "Limited" or "Ltd". Exact text nearby: "You must include your company's name on all company documents, publicity and letters."
- Page dates: first published 3 Jul 2012, last public update 29 Jan 2025, last touched 23 Oct 2026. It is standing government guidance, not a statistic, so the 24-month rule is met by the page being current.
- How the frames use it. Frame 2: "Those three letters come with a list" (the list is the four items above). Frame 3: "Your website has to show all four". The two plates in frame 3 show the four as an example: "Ltd" in the name (the fact that it is a limited company), "Registered in England and Wales" (where it is registered), "Company No." (the registered number) and "Registered office" (the address).
- What the frames do not say. They do not say where on the page the details must go: "Check your footer tonight" is a prompt to look, and the footer is only the usual place. They do not say a door sign needs these details (the same GOV.UK page asks only for a sign showing the company name). They give no legal advice and name no penalty. The same rule covers business letters and order forms; the Story only talks about websites.

## The made-up business

Kittiwake & Quay Ltd is invented for this Story. Web searches in this run for "Kittiwake & Quay" and "Kittiwake and Quay Limited" found no company of that name (there are companies called Kittiwake Limited and others with Kittiwake in the name, none with this name). "12 Quay Row, Sunderland" is a made-up address: a search for "Quay Row" Sunderland found no such street. The company number 24601357 is a dummy: Companies House numbers for England and Wales have not reached the 24 million range (this was not looked up in this run; it is from general knowledge, and the number is only set dressing on a made-up plate).

Self-check: the frames say nothing beyond the claim above. No "small business", no person named, no orange (brass is drawn as yellow gold, #E6CB72 for the accent).
