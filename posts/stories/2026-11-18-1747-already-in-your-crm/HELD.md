**What the reviewers said**
- Both liked the idea, the render (paper curl, shadows), the blue, yellow and white palette and the headline type. Neither called a frame basic. Both named frame 2 the weakest.
- Round 1 (6.5): frame 2 had the top sheet flipped back over the binding, a blank pale curl behind Instagram's top buttons; the carbon writing looked like a low-resolution texture; words on the form cut by the left edge in frame 3; the sign-off row (URL and wordmark on one line) was called template-grade; the blue differed from frame to frame; headings on the sheets too faint.
- Fixed for round 2: frame 2 re-shot with the top sheet torn out; carbon made crisper; the key light moved back so the three blues match; headings on the sheets darkened; the pen's emboss taken off the back of the sheet; frame 3 pulled back so every word is whole; the URL set alone and larger.
- Round 2 (6.5): frame 2 is now "one object, one headline, empty lower third" and the pad's top sits under the top buttons; the carbon writing still reads as blurred beside the crisp red number; every pad crop is a near miss with the frame edge (a sliver of a corner cut, or 20px clear) instead of a clear bleed or a clear margin; frames 1 and 3 share a layout; the form's last line sits in the bottom band in frames 1 and 3; no wordmark on any frame and the call to action is a thin line of text; the pad corner is 35px above "Nobody.".

**If it is reworked**
- Keep frame 1 (the strongest both times). Frame 2 needs both sheets in it, not the copy alone: lift the top sheet with the copy sharp under it, and keep any pale paper out of the top 250px.
- Carbon: one clean, even blue-violet line at the same sharpness as the print. Mottle and blur read as a bad texture at phone size.
- Decide every crop: the pad either bleeds a long way off an edge or sits 60px clear of it. Keep all form content above y 1600.
- Last frame: a wordmark, and the call to action as large as the object's title.
- Told the round 1 reviewer the bands were 220 and 380px (the Reel figures) by mistake; Stories are 250 and 320px. Round 2 used the right figures and the score was the same.
