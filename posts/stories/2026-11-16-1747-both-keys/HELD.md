# Held: not published

This Story was made by the 17:47 run on Mon 16 Nov 2026 and **was not published**. Do not publish it from a scheduled run. It needs Lee's say-so, or a rework and a fresh design review that passes.

**Why.** The independent design review scored it 6 out of 10, twice (pass mark 7). Both reviewers named frame 2 (the close-up of the .co.uk tag) as the weakest. The Story task says two failed rounds means nothing goes out, so the slot was left empty. DESIGN.md's "Always post" rule is written for feed slots, so it was not applied here (same call as the 13 Nov squares-or-curves Story).

**The idea.** Domain names, this or that: .co.uk or .com? Two brass keys for a made-up florist (Wrenfield), each with a manila key tag, rendered in 3D (Blender, `art/build.py`; tags drawn by `art/tex.py`) on a graphite slab. Anton headlines, Geist text, one ice blue (#A9D7F2) for the UK ending.
- Frame 1: both keys and tags side by side, wrenfield.co.uk and wrenfield.com. ".co.uk or .com?"
- Frame 2: nearer and higher on the .co.uk tag, on the diagonal. "It says UK. Visitors can tell they're dealing with a British business."
- Frame 3: both keys on one split ring, both tags fanned. "Get both. Point the .com at your .co.uk, so no one else can have it." + wordmark.
Sources: `sources.md` (names.co.uk guide, opened during the run).

**What the reviewers said**
- Both liked the idea, the arc (question, reason, answer), one margin, one accent, and the type staying clear of Instagram's bars.
- Round 1 (6): the domain on the tags looked like a flat decal in "a generic heavy font"; frame 2 was mostly out-of-focus manila and the dots in ".co.uk" read as underscores; full stops tight on "UK." and "both."; headline tops not matched; logo not aligned.
- Fixed for round 2: the tags re-stamped letter by letter (rubber letter set: each letter turned, offset and inked on its own), both endings one size; frame 2 re-shot higher and on the diagonal so the dots read round; slab darkened to match across frames; full stops opened; frames 2 and 3 share a headline top.
- Round 2 (6): the hero still gives it away. The uneven per-letter ink read as "an AI-generated texture" and the condensed face as cheap; the print is sharp while the card around it is soft, so it looks pasted on; frame 2 crops through a blown-out ring at the top and has a big empty lower half; keys differ between frames (one glows, one matte); frame 1's headline is about 40px under the right tag; the second subtitle line on frames 2 and 3 ends about 10px above the reply bar.

**If it is reworked**
- Tag lettering: print it evenly (one clean impression, or a typed label) and keep the print inside the depth of field of the card around it, or give the whole tag the same focus.
- Frame 2: crop the ring fully out (or fully in), fill the empty lower half (show the .com tag behind, out of focus), and raise the text block 60 to 80px.
- Keep the same brass and light on both keys in every frame; move frame 1's headline down or the tags up.
