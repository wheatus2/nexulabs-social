Every page of your website can have a page title: a short line of text that says what the page is. It's one of the things Google uses to write the title of your search result, and Google's own guidance says to avoid vague ones like "Home".

Say what the page is about, keep it short, make each page's title different, and put your business name at the start or end.

Google can still show its own wording if it thinks your title doesn't fit the page, so a clear one gives you more say in what people see.

Not sure what yours say? Ask whoever looks after your site.
