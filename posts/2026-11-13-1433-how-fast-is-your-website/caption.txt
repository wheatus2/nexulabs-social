Web design check for North East businesses: how fast is your website?

Google draws two lines. If the main part of a page is on screen within 2.5 seconds, it calls that good. Past 4 seconds, it calls it poor. And it wants the good time to hold for most of your visitors, on phones as well as desktops.

You don't have to guess where yours sits. Go to pagespeed.web.dev, which is Google's own test, and type in your web address.

If it comes back slow, send the result to whoever built the site and ask what's holding it up.
