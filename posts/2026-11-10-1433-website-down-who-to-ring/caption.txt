The worst time to find out who hosts your website is when it's down.

It's easy to lose track of. Maybe the site was set up years ago, the person who built it has moved on, and nobody's sure where it lives or whose card pays for it.

While everything's working, write four things down: who hosts the site, who holds the login, when it renews and how it's paid, and the support number or email. Note who has the login, not the password itself.

If you can't fill one in, that's the one to chase this week.
