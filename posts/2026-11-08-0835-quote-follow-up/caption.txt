Sending a quote is the easy bit. The hard part is what happens after, when the customer goes quiet and it sits in your sent folder.

A decent CRM gives every quote a status and a reminder. Sent, chased, won or lost. When the reminder pops up you give them a quick call, note it on the job and move on. If it's a no, write down why.

Keep that up for a few months and you'll know whether it's price, timing or something else costing you work.
