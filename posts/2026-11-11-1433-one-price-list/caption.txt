Put your prices up and it's easy to miss a place. The new figure goes on the invoice, but the website and the order form still show the old one.

If your website and your CRM share one price list, you change it once. The site, the order form and the invoice all follow.

Worth doing tonight: pick three prices on your website and check them against what you charge now.
