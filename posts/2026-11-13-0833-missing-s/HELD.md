# Held: not published

This carousel was made by the 08:33 run on Fri 13 Nov 2026 and **was not published**. Do not publish it from a scheduled run. It needs Lee's say-so, or a rework and a fresh design review that passes.

**Why.** The independent design review scored it 6.5 out of 10, twice (pass mark 7), and both reviewers plainly called frame 2 basic. Two failed rounds means nothing goes out (DESIGN.md), so the 08:33 slot was left empty.

**The idea.** Hosting: a web address that starts http is missing its s. Hollow letters mean missing. Frame 1 sets "http" across the frame with the s as an empty outline; frame 2 is Chrome's label, "Not secure"; frame 3 fills the gap with one very large solid s ("Ask your host for the s."). Sky blue, Nexulabs blue and ink, Space Grotesk.

**What the reviewers said**
- Both liked the idea, the two-colour palette and the matching margins, footer and logo. Frame 3 was the strongest both times (about 7.5). Frame 1 was "plain but not basic".
- Frame 2, round 1 (a drawn browser window with a large "Not secure" chip in the address bar): "a generic skeleton mock-up of the kind every SaaS template ships with", mixed scales, the only frame with a shadow.
- Frame 2, round 2 (rebuilt in type: "Not" solid over "secure" hollow, then "That's what Chrome tells your customers."): "only words on a flat colour", two headlines competing, and it "describes the Chrome warning instead of showing it". So one reviewer wanted the chip gone and the next wanted it back.
- Across the set, round 2: display type at 250px and over is set with default letter fit. Uneven gaps in "https" (h to t about 15px, t to p about 32px), the two t crossbars merging into one slab, "o" nearly touching "t" in "Not", the "r" arm close to the "e" in "secure".
- Frame 3: the big s is cropped indecisively by the right edge (bleed it boldly or keep it inside); "http" and the s read as two objects.
- Frames 1 and 3: the "y" of "your" sits 13 to 16px above the letters on the next line; open the leading a little more.

**If it is reworked**
- Frame 2 is the problem. Try the real thing: a properly made picture of Chrome's own "Not secure" address bar or its warning page, at full-frame scale, in the post's colours. Or cut to two frames (1 and 3) and post it as a short carousel on an evening slot.
- Kern the big words by hand (one element per letter, or SVG with a dx per letter) so every gap is equal by eye.
- Frame 3: push the s further off the edge, or shrink it to sit inside the margin, and bring "http" closer.

**What is fine.** The facts passed the independent fact-check (see sources.md), including a recent one worth using again: Google said on 21 Oct 2026 that Chrome now asks for permission before opening a site without HTTPS, on by default for everyone. `studio.py check` passes. The caption is final. Not in design-log.json, so the palette and typeface are not used up.
