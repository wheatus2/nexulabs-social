## Claim 1: Google lets you set special hours for days like Christmas Eve and Christmas Day without changing your normal week
- Used on: scene 4 ("Set special hours.") and the caption.
- Source: Google Business Profile Help, "How to set special hours". https://support.google.com/business/answer/6303076?hl=en-GB
- As the source states it: "When your hours of operation change for a brief period of time, like for a special event, you can set special hours so that your regular hours don't change." Under "How it works": "When necessary, you can schedule special opening hours for:" "Holidays", "Special events", "Other exceptional circumstances".
- Second source: Google Business Profile Help, "Edit your opening hours". https://support.google.com/business/answer/15300403?hl=en-GB : "For days when you have unusual hours, such as holidays or special events, you can set special hours."
- Date: neither page shows a date. Both live when opened on 16 Nov 2026. UK English (en-GB) versions of Google's own help pages.
- Note: the pages say "holidays"; Christmas Eve and Christmas Day are our examples of such days. The source also says special hours cover a business that "remains closed for up to six days in a row"; we make no claim about longer closures.

## Claim 2: where to find it: Business Profile, Edit profile, then Hours
- Used on: scene 4 ("Business Profile › Edit profile › Hours") and the caption ("It's in your Business Profile: Edit profile, then Hours.").
- Source: "How to set special hours" (as above), under "Set special hours": "Go to your Business Profile." "Select Edit profile and then Hours." "In the 'Opening hours' section, next to 'Special hours', select Edit." Then select the date, update the hours (or tick "Closed"), and "Select Save."

## Claim 3: Google suggests confirming hours for official holidays even when they are the same as usual
- Used on: caption.
- Source: "How to set special hours" (as above): "It's a good idea to confirm your hours for official holidays, even if they're the same as your regular hours." "This makes it clear to customers that your special hours are accurate."
- This is Google's suggestion, not a requirement; the caption says "suggests".

## Claim 4: Google shows opening hours for a business
