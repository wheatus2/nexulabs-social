If someone's never used you before, there's a good chance they'll have a look at your website before they pick up the phone.

A lot of sites make that harder than it needs to be. The reviews are on their own page, the insurance details are buried in the footer, and the photos are stock.

Move the proof up to where people are already looking. A line of reviews under the headline, what you're insured for next to the button, and a few photos of jobs you've actually done.
