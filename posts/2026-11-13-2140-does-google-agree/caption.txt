Website and Google check for North East businesses: your door says open. Does Google?

Search your business name on your phone and read the opening hours Google shows. If you've claimed your Business Profile, that's where you set and edit them.

Christmas Day is about six weeks away. Google lets you set special hours for days like that without changing your normal week. It also suggests confirming your hours for official holidays even when they're the same as usual, so customers can see they're right.

Then make sure the hours on your own website say the same.
