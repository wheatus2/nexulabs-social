CRM for trades in the North East starts with the phone. It rang at 2pm, you were on a job, and nobody wrote it down.

A one-minute check tonight: open your call log and look for today's missed numbers. Ring them back, or send a text if it's late.

Then make it a habit. Every missed call goes on one list with a name, what they wanted and a time to ring back. A notebook will do. A CRM that reminds you is better.
