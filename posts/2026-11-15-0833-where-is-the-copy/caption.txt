If your website vanished tonight, could you put it back?

The NCSC's guide to backing up names your website alongside your emails, invoices and customer details as things your business needs a copy of. It also suggests keeping two copies, one online and one on a separate device, in case one is lost or fails.

The step people skip is putting a copy back. The NCSC says to make sure you know how to restore a backup, and to check it holds everything.

So ask whoever hosts your site: how often is it copied, where does the copy live, and when did anyone last try restoring it?
