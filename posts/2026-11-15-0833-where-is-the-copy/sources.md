Source: National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC), Small organisations guide to cyber security, "Backing up your data"

Published 9 April 2026, reviewed 21 July 2026. UK government guidance.

## Claim 1 (caption): the NCSC's guide names your website among the things your business needs a copy of
- "make a copy (also known as a backup) of all the data that your business needs to operate"
- "This could include your website, emails, invoicing, documents, presentations, contacts and customer information."

## Claim 2 (caption): it suggests keeping two copies, one online and one on a separate device, in case one is lost or fails
- "consider using online and a storage device to create two backups, in case one of them is lost or stolen, or if the storage device fails."

## Claim 3 (frame 3, caption): know how to restore a backup, and check it holds everything
- "Once you've made your backup, it's important you know how to restore it and to check that it contains all your important data."

## Advice with no outside fact (frames 1 and 2, caption)
- "Where's the copy of your website?", "When was it last copied?", "Ask whoever hosts it how often, and where the copy is kept": questions for the reader, not claims.
- The floppy disk, its label and the date on it are a made-up prop; the caption says so.
