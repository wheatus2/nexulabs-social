Not claims: scene 2 is an example screen (labelled "Example screen.") for a placeholder domain, yourbusiness.co.uk. "Email: not in your control" and "Check yours in five minutes" are our own practical framing: if the domain isn't yours, the email addresses on it depend on whoever holds it. "Or ask whoever set it up" (scene 4 step 2) is practical advice, not a fact claim.

Left out on purpose: day counts for expiry/suspension/cancellation (the Nominet page is from 2019 and .uk standardisation may have changed the lifecycle), and the £10 + VAT Nominet transfer fee (The UK Domain says direct management through Nominet Online Services ends 9 Feb 2027, after which registrants go through a registrar).
