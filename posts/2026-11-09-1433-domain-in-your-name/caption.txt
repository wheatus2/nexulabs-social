Whose name is on your domain?

It's worth checking. When a web designer or IT firm registers a domain for you, it can end up in their name, with their email on it. Then the renewal reminders go to them, and moving it to you usually means they have to start the transfer.

For a .uk or .co.uk name, Nominet's domain lookup shows which registrar it's with. Log in there and check the registrant is your business, spelled properly, with an email you'll always have. If it isn't, ask for a registrant transfer.
