Have you tried turning it off and on again?

Microsoft 365 went down on Thursday and took Outlook and Teams with it. In fairness, restarting things was on the list: its status updates said it was "actively restarting and validating the affected servers". Some then fell over again, and it was still being put right on Friday.

If it can happen to Microsoft, spare a minute for your own plan B. If your email went down tomorrow, could a customer still reach you? A phone number that's easy to find on your website is a good start. So is a contact form that saves each enquiry as well as emailing it.
