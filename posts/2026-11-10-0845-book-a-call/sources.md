# Sources

Our own product: the Book a call tool on nexulabs.co.uk (built and released 9 November 2026).

- /book/: free days and times, phone or video (Microsoft Teams, Google Meet or Zoom), name, email and what it's about. Checked on the live site.
- Calls are 30 minutes on weekday evenings (the booking admin's settings at release, 6pm to 8pm UK time, 24 hours' notice).
- The confirmation email carries a calendar invite and a cancel link; "Before our call" asks the Start a project questions.
- Bookings are kept on our own server and deleted 90 days after the call (privacy policy, 9 November 2026).
