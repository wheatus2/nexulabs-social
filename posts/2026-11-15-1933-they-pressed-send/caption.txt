Web design tip from the North East: what does your website do after someone presses Send?

If the answer is nothing, and the boxes just clear, your customer is left wondering whether it went.

Two lines fix that. Say you've got their message, and say when you'll reply. The guidance GOV.UK writes for government services says a confirmation page must give details of what happens next and when, and a reference number if there is one.
