## Claim 1 (caption)
**Claim:** The guidance GOV.UK writes for government services says a confirmation page must give details of what happens next and when, and a reference number if there is one.
**Source:** GOV.UK Design System, "Confirmation pages" pattern: https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/confirmation-pages
**What the source says:** "Confirmation pages reassure users that they have completed a transaction and helps them understand what to expect next." "Your confirmation page must include:" "a reference number, if there is one"; "details of what happens next and when"; "contact details for the service" (the list goes on: links users are likely to need next, a link to the GOV.UK feedback page, a way to save a record).
**Source date:** the page shows no publish or updated date; it is the current, live pattern in the GOV.UK Design System (opened 15 Nov 2026).
**Notes:** UK source. It is guidance for government services, and the caption says so. We do not say it is a rule for business websites.
