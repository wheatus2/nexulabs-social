## Idea
A contact form as a chrome counter bell on a lagoon-blue counter, rendered in Blender (`art/build.py`, slip print `art/slip.py`). They pressed Send (the button goes down twice). / Did it go? The form just cleared. / Tell them you've got it (a till slip: RECEIVED, WE'LL REPLY BY 10AM MON). / Say when. Syne 800, deep ink type. The finished draft is `reel.mp4` (15.4s); the first version is in `art/v1/`.

## Round 1 (5/10), `art/v1/`
Weakest: the end card. The payoff line "10AM MON" sat in Instagram's bottom 380px, a blurred RECEIVED filled the middle, the logo was jammed over the headline. Also: bell cropped by the right edge with its foot in the bottom band, dome reflections "hard-edged white polygons that read as CG", foot "grey plastic", slip corner looked as if it cut into the bell, headline sizes and left margins differed from scene to scene.

## Round 2 (6/10), this version
Changed: every subject lifted clear of the bottom band, bell whole in scene 1, soft reflection panels in place of hard ones, satin black foot, thicker stem and a skirted collar, a shorter slip lying clear of the bell so the reply time is sharp and above the band, looser line spacing.
Weakest: scene 2, the close-up of the button: "the macro exposes the model", stepped reflections on the stem, the collar "hovers over a black hole", the bottom third "murky". Scene 1 was called basic: "a stock-looking chrome bell on a flat colour with a headline above". Scene 3 (bell and slip) was called the strongest.
Also named: bell not centred in scene 1; the end of "Tell them" close to the bell in scene 3; slip clipped at both sides in scene 4; headline sizes still vary; "every frame is the same flat teal".

## What to take from it
- Both reviewers liked the idea and the script. Both said the 3D gives it away. A simple lathe-turned chrome object is "stock" however it is lit: chrome needs a real room to mirror (an HDRI or a built set), not panels.
- A close-up of a simple model shows its simplicity. Go close only on a part that has real detail (print, engraving, wear).
- The frame that scored best had two objects in a relationship (bell and slip). Start there next time and open on it.
- Reviewers mark down anything that matters in the bottom 380px even when told the picture shows through. Compose for the middle 1,320px from the first draft.
- Syne 800 is very wide: 152px is the most a seven-letter word takes on a 1080 frame, which forces a different size in every scene. Pick a narrower face when the lines differ in length.
