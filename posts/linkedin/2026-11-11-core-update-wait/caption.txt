If your website has moved up or down in Google this week, don't rush to change it.

Google started rolling out its November 2026 core update on Tuesday 10 November. It's a broad change to how search results are ranked, it applies worldwide and in every language, and Google says it may take up to two weeks to finish.

Google's own advice to site owners is to wait. Let the update finish, give it at least another full week, then use Search Console to compare a week after it ended with a week before it started. Look at your top pages and the searches they show up for.

Google gives two examples. A page slipping from position 2 to 4 needs no drastic action, and Google recommends leaving content that's already doing well alone. A drop from 4 to 29 calls for a deeper look at the whole site and the pages most affected. It also warns against quick fixes, and says deleting content is a last resort.
