Sending a quote is the easy part. Knowing which ones have gone quiet is where work slips away.

The quote goes out, the customer doesn't reply, and it sits in a sent folder until they ring back or book someone else. Nobody decided to drop it. There just wasn't anything telling you to pick it up again.

A decent CRM sorts that by giving every quote a status: sent, chased, won or lost. Each one gets a reminder date and a name next to it, so the oldest open quotes sit at the top of the list.

Our rule of thumb is a quick check on day three that it arrived, then a call after a week, noting what was said each time. No answer at two weeks? Ask whether it's going ahead and close it off.

When one's won, it becomes the job without anyone retyping it. When you lose one, write down why. After a few months you'll see whether it's price, timing or something else costing you work.

That's the kind of quote tracking we build into CRMs for trades and service businesses.
