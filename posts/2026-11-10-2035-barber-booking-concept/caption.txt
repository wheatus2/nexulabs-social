If your website makes people ring to book, some of them won't.

This is a concept we put together for a barbershop. The booking box is the first thing on the page: pick a service, pick a time, confirm. Prices sit right there too, so nobody has to call to ask. And it's designed for a phone first, because that's where most people will open it.

Harbour Barbers is a made-up business. The thinking works for any trade that runs on appointments.
