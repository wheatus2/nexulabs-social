On 4 November Microsoft announced Agentic Analytics for Clarity, its free website analytics tool. Its Agent Activity report shows which AI agents visit your site and what they do there: searches, form submissions, bookings, checkout.

Microsoft's own data says weekly sessions from AI agents nearly doubled in three weeks, to almost 2 million, between August and September. Those are its figures from 230,511 Clarity projects, not UK-only.

Worth a look if people book or enquire through your site. You'll see whether the agents get as far as your form.

One UK catch: Clarity needs cookie consent from UK visitors to work fully, so link it to your banner.
