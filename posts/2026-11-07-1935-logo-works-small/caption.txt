A logo usually gets designed on a big screen, then spends the rest of its life tiny.

It's the round profile picture on Instagram and WhatsApp, the icon in a browser tab, the corner of a quote you've emailed over. If there's a long name, a tagline and a little drawing all in there, it turns into a smudge at that size.

Worth a quick test tonight. Shrink yours down to about the size of your thumbnail and see if you'd still know whose it is.

If it doesn't hold up, you don't always need a new logo. A simpler version for small spaces often does the job.
