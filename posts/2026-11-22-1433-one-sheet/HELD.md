One made-up concertina leaflet standing on a red paper sweep, rendered in Blender: white card, seven pages, a black numeral and a name on each (Website, Enquiries, Quotes, Bookings, Jobs, Hosting, then the wordmark) and one red line through every fold. "Your website is page one." / "The CRM is pages two to five." / "Both built as one." / "See what we build at nexulabs.co.uk".

## For next time
- A standing concertina is a wide object. In a 9:16 frame it only fills the picture two or three pages at a time, and any view that shows both sets of pages is square on. That is what made the middle scene basic twice. A wide object wants a carousel (4:5), not a Reel.
- The closed leaflet with its long shadow (first frame, last frame) and the opening were the best things in it both times: 7.
- An in-between position of a fold shows cut numerals and words whatever is done. Either cut straight from open to shut, or print nothing near the page edges.
- Light along the even pages: shade then starts exactly at the folds and nothing printed is cut by a shadow edge.
- Timings on four cores: a 1134x2016 frame at 110 samples in about 3 minutes, in-betweens at 40 samples in about 1.5; the long track picture (2394x2016) about 8.
