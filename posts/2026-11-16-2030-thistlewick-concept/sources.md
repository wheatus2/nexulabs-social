## The research the design was based on (read 16 Nov 2026, each opened from a web search in this session)
Not quoted in the post; listed so the design choices can be traced.
- Nielsen Norman Group, "Homepage Design: 5 Fundamental Principles": https://www.nngroup.com/articles/homepage-design-principles/ (say what the site does at once; logo top left; visible navigation; buttons labelled with the outcome)
- Nielsen Norman Group, "Scrolling and Attention": https://www.nngroup.com/articles/scrolling-and-attention/ (most viewing time goes to the first screen)
- web.dev, "Accessible tap targets": https://web.dev/articles/accessible-tap-targets (buttons about 48px, with space between)
- web.dev, "Accessible responsive design": https://web.dev/articles/accessible-responsive-design (text in rem; do not disable zoom)
- Hook Agency, "Home service website examples": https://hookagency.com/blog/home-service-website-examples/ (service and area in the first screen; phone and button top right; fixed call bar on phones)
- Synqro, "Best websites": https://synqro.co/blog/best-websites (one idea per screen; one primary action; a limited palette; type carrying the identity)
- Hon Tran, "Best award-winning websites 2026": https://www.hontran.dev/blog/best-award-winning-websites-2026 (the first screen must hold up as a still)
- Digidop, web design trends: https://www.digidop.com/blog/5-web-design-trends-2025 ; Webflow, "Homepage optimization strategies": https://webflow.com/blog/homepage-optimization-strategies
The design is our own; no real website's layout was copied.
