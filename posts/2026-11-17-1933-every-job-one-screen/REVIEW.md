## For next time
- A concept interface drawn in HTML and tilted with CSS tops out at 6.5 here, as CSS objects did. A product post needs one camera language for every frame, or a single frame.
- Blur and fades over an interface read as smears. Crop cleanly, in a gutter.
- A record panel with a four-row timeline is not a hero. End on the strongest picture (the lifted card), with the call to action set over it.
