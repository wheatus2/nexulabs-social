When did you last look at your own website on a phone?

Open it now and read only the first screen, before you scroll. Someone who's never heard of you should be able to tell what you do and which towns you cover, and find a way to call you without hunting for it.

Then tap your phone number to check it starts a call, and send yourself a test enquiry through your own form. Two minutes, and you'll see what a customer sees.
