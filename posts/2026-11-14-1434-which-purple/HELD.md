# Held: not published

This Reel was made by the 14:33 scheduled run on Sat 14 Nov 2026. It **was not published**: the independent design review scored it 6 out of 10 twice (pass mark 7), and the scheduled task says to publish nothing after two failed rounds. It is finished (rendered, `studio.py check` passes, facts checked and passed, caption final). It needs Lee's say-so, or the rework below and a fresh review.

**The idea.** Know your colour codes. "What colour is your logo?" A paint-shop colour fan deck answers "Purple." It fans open: the same word on five different purples (logo, van, sign, website, uniform). "Which purple?" Then, close on the open fan, the top leaf gives its code, #5B2A86, with "This purple." printed across the colour itself.

**The two reviews: 6 and 6.**
1. First design: five loose colour cards on plum paper, dealt one over the next (not kept: the build scripts were rewritten for the second design). 6. "A tidy small-studio template." The card that said #5B2A86 did not measure #5B2A86 in the picture; scenes 1 and 3 were one shot; the small labels could not be read; the 3D was called flat (no material, texture or real shadow); the end card was called basic.
2. Second design, a new object: the riveted fan deck, the top leaf graded to measure its own code, the labels three times the size, the payoff line printed on the leaf, a focus pull. 6 again. "The idea is at that level, the execution is not." The fan frame alone was given 7.5. Marked down: flat lighting and almost no shadows, so the white bands merge into one shape and the deck does not sit on the paper; the payoff frame's deck is small in the frame and its line smaller than the question before it; the focus pull barely reads; the end card was called basic again (it has since been cut, and the wordmark moved under the deck in scene 3).

**If it is reworked.** Keep the object and the script. Light it from low on the camera's side with a small source, tilt each leaf a touch so the tips lift and throw shadows on the leaf below, and give the deck a contact shadow. Shoot the payoff much closer, with the rivet end bleeding off the left, so "This purple." is as wide as "Which purple?". Replace the focus pull with the top leaf's print changing from "Purple." to the code. `art/build.py` renders everything (`python3 build.py all final`, about 50 minutes on two cores); `art/cards.py` prints the leaf faces first.
