What colour is your logo? Brand colours, for North East businesses.

If the answer is one word, like "purple", everyone else has to guess. The signwriter, the printer and the web designer can each land on a different purple, and the van stops matching the website.

A code takes the guessing out. On a website a colour is often written as a hex code, a # and six characters. Printers mostly work in CMYK, or a Pantone number when the colour has to stay the same from job to job. The NHS lists its blue as Pantone 300, hex #005EB8.

Ask whoever made your logo for your codes and keep them handy. The colour deck is made up.
