## For next time
- A flat white envelope is too plain an object to carry three frames. It needs a flap, a postmark, a crease and raking light, or a different object.
- A middle frame that rearranges the objects of frame 1 scores 5 however it is arranged. It needs something new in the picture.
- Fanning rectangles from one exact corner makes wedges. Overlap them loosely.
- Tools: this container's `python3` is 3.11 but pip installs to 3.13, so bpy and playwright run under `python3.13`; the pip playwright wanted chromium build 1255 and the container has 1243 (symlinked in /opt/pw-browsers for this run).
