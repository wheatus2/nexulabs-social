Google started rolling out its November 2026 core update on Tuesday. It's a broad change to how search results are ranked, it applies worldwide, and Google says it may take up to two weeks to finish.

So if your website moves up or down in Google over the next fortnight, that could be why.

Google's own advice is to wait until the update has finished, give it another full week, then compare your numbers in Search Console. A small drop needs no drastic action. A big one calls for a proper look at your content, and Google says to avoid quick fixes.
