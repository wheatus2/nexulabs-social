# Growth brief (11 Nov 2026, starting point)

**Where we are:** 8 followers. Feed posts reach 1 to 7 accounts; the best Reel reached 29. These are early signs, not findings.

**Make more of**
- Reels. They are the only format reaching people who don't follow us yet. Give the first second the whole idea.
- Posts a North East business owner would send to someone: "is your site doing this?" checks they can try on their own website in a minute.
- Concept sites and screens for recognisable local trades.

**Make less of**
- Posts that only make sense if you already know Nexulabs.

**Three ideas**
1. A Reel: "Search your own business name. What shows up?" in three beats.
2. A carousel concept site for a North East trade (a coastal café, a Sunderland salon, a Durham builder).
3. A Reel showing an enquiry going from a website form to a CRM lead in real time.

**Captions:** use the plain phrases people search for, naturally, in the first two lines: web design North East, website for [trade], custom CRM.

**Hashtags to rotate (3 to 5 a post):** #northeastbusiness #sunderlandbusiness #newcastlebusiness #durhambusiness #webdesignuk #websitedesign #customcrm #northeastengland

**End on:** one clear reason to follow, for example "Follow for one website fix a day."
