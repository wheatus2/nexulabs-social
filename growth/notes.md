# Growth research notes

## 11 Nov 2026 (evening): hashtags and search phrases

Rotation: 1 of 5 (next evening: local accounts, groups and events).

**How Instagram treats hashtags now**
- Posts and Reels are capped at 5 hashtags. Instagram announced it on 18 Dec 2025 and rolled it out gradually. Its own line: fewer, more targeted hashtags beat many generic ones. [1][2]
- Adam Mosseri has said hashtags don't raise reach; they tell people and Instagram what a post is about. [1] So a hashtag is a label, not a distribution lever.
- Extra tags in the first comment are no longer a workaround: a comment with more than 5 reportedly won't post. Treat 5 as the total. (Secondary source [2].)
- Search leans on the words in the caption. Several guides say caption keywords are indexed and that public posts have appeared in Google since July 2025. [2][3] I could not open Instagram's own help pages tonight, so this is secondary, though it matches what GROWTH.md already says.

**Which North East tags are worth a slot** (sizes are from third-party trackers, so rough)
- `#northeastengland`: about 152,000 posts. Specific to us and big enough to be browsed. Keep. [4]
- `#northeast`: about 1.9 million, but dominated by North East India. Drop it. [4]
- `#sunderland` about 787,000, `#newcastleupontyne` about 746,000: usable when the post is about that place. `#newcastle` (5.6 million) and `#durham` (2.3 million) are too broad to mean anything. [5]
- `#nebiz`: 1,524 posts and none per hour on a tracker last updated Nov 2024. Not worth a slot. [5]
- Tags that tracker shows local firms using together: `#northeastbusiness`, `#sunderlandbusiness`, `#durhambusiness`, `#tyneandwear`, `#northeastcreatives`, `#sunderlandcreatives`, `#wearesunderlandcreatives`. No sizes shown, so they are unverified. [5]
- `#LoveDurham` and `#DurhamUK` are Visit County Durham's tourism tags. Only for a post that is really about a Durham place. [6]

**Not Instagram, but noted**
- `#NorthEastHour` is a networking hour on X (Twitter): Mondays 8 to 9pm and Tuesdays 2 to 3pm. `#nefollowers` is Thursdays 2 to 3pm. Visit County Durham still lists both (page updated Apr 2026). I did not check how busy they are now, and Nexulabs would need to be on X to use them. Parked. [6][7]

**What I could not check**
- The real size and freshness of `#northeastbusiness`, `#sunderlandbusiness`, `#newcastlebusiness` and `#durhambusiness` on Instagram itself (its pages can't be opened from here). Lee can see each count in ten seconds by typing the tag into Instagram search. Worth asking once in a morning list.

**For the morning brief**
- 3 or 4 tags a post, never more than 5: one place (`#northeastengland`, or `#sunderland` / `#newcastleupontyne` when the post is about there), one local business tag (`#northeastbusiness` or `#sunderlandbusiness`), one topic (`#webdesignuk`, `#customcrm`), and sometimes `#northeastcreatives`. Take `#northeast` out of rotation if it creeps in.
- The search phrase does more than the tag. Put it in plain words in the first line of the caption and in the alt text: "web design Sunderland", "website for a salon in Durham", "CRM for trades in the North East".
